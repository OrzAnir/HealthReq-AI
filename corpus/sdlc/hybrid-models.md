# Hybrid models

Hybrid models combine the strengths of two models.

Agile plus V-Model (sometimes called Agile-V): development happens in Agile sprints, while each increment still passes through V-Model style verification and validation, with traceability from requirements to tests and formal reviews before release. This suits regulated domains whose requirements still evolve.

Agile plus DevSecOps: Agile planning and sprints, with a DevSecOps pipeline that automates testing, security scanning and deployment, plus human approval gates for high-risk releases.

Waterfall plus Agile: a fixed, documented requirements and design phase, followed by iterative Agile implementation.

Source: SDLC reference notes written for this project, summarising standard software engineering textbook material (Sommerville; Pressman).
